One-line: a single number with its unit, change and basis — the Home and Progress summary tile.

```jsx
<StatCard label="Volume" value="12,450" unit="kg" delta="+8%" footnote="vs last week" icon="trending-up" />
```

Numerals use tabular figures so a pair of cards side by side stays aligned. Always give the delta a basis — a percentage with no comparison is noise.
