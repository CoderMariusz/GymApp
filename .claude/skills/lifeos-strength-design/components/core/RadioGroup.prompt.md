# RadioGroup

One choice out of three to six, where each option needs a word of explanation — theme, language,
weight increment. Rows are 52px, stacked with an 8px gap, and the selected row takes a lime border
plus a filled dot: two signals, not one (C-4).

Two to four short labels with no explanation belong in `SegmentedControl` instead; more than six
options belong in `Select`.
